Apply to Become a Brews Beyond Borders Scholarship Recipient

Each year, Brews Beyond Borders selects a community to invest in, providing resources, funding, and support to foster local brewing and fermentation initiatives.

Share the challenges your community faces and explain how our assistance could make a meaningful difference. Selected communities gain access to specialized courses and funding to further our mission of spreading fermentation and beer culture on a global scale.

Together, we can empower local economies, celebrate cultural traditions, and build a stronger global brewing community.
